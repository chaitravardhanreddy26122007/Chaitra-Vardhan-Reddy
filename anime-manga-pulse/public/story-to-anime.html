Content Moderation & Anti-Bot Safeguards

Animorphia is built as a clean, creative storytelling studio. We adhere to two strict technical guidelines:

1. Restrict Nudity & NSFW Content

Real-time lexical filters analyze story input to ensure zero nudity, non-consensual themes, or explicit adult content. Generation is automatically halted if prohibited keywords are identified.

2. Restrict AI Bots & Web Scrapers

Equipped with strict robots.txt, noai, and noimageai headers. Headless automation tools and automated AI web scrapers are blocked from harvesting content.
